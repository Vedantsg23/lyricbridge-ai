How it works

  1. 0:00

    Drop the song in

    MP3, WAV, M4A or a video's soundtrack, in any language. The file stays on your device the whole time.

    ♪ your-song.mp3 3:12

    Any language · up to 10 minutes

  2. 0:04

    Whisper picks up the lyrics

    OpenAI's Whisper runs right in your browser. It works out the language and writes down every line with its timestamp.

    0:04 Bajo la luna te esperé

    0:08 con el corazón en la mano

    0:13 y me importa un pepino el qué dirán

    Detected: Spanish · 97%

  3. 0:13

    Meaning, not just words

    Idioms and slang are translated for what they mean, with a short note whenever a phrase has no English twin.

    me importa un pepino

    a cucumber matters to me

    I couldn't care less

    Idiom: not caring at all

  4. 3:12

    Subtitles, ready to go

    Play the song with synced bilingual lyrics, fix any line by hand, then download SRT or VTT subtitles for your video.

    3
00:00:13,400 --> 00:00:19,800
y me importa un pepino el qué dirán
and I couldn't care less what people say

    SRTVTT

    Lines that crossed the bridge

    Example lines written for this demo, to show meaning winning over word-for-word.

    • Portuguese

      Saudade é o nome dessa canção

      Longing is the name of this song

      Saudade: a deep, nostalgic longing with no single English word.

    • German

      Ich verstehe nur Bahnhof

      It's all Greek to me

      Literally “I only understand train station”.

    • Hindi

      बारिश में भीगी शाम, तेरा नाम

      A rain-soaked evening, and your name

    • French

      Il pleut des cordes, je danse quand même

      It's pouring, and I'm dancing anyway

      “It's raining ropes” is French for heavy rain.

    • Japanese

      夜明けまで歌おう

      Let's sing until dawn

      Showers of rain, and the memory of you

      별빛 아래 우리 둘

      Just the two of us, under starlight

    • Spanish

      Eres mi media naranja

      You're my other half

      Literally “my half orange”.
